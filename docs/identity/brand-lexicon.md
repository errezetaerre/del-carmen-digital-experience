Objective
The Brand Lexicon defines the official meaning of the concepts that shape the Del Carmen universe.
Its purpose is to ensure that every designer, developer, writer, curator, collaborator and AI system interprets the brand language consistently across every experience.
The Lexicon serves as a permanent reference that preserves the philosophical integrity of Del Carmen as the ecosystem evolves.

Governance
The Brand Lexicon is a living document.
New concepts may be incorporated only when they represent a meaningful addition to the philosophy of Del Carmen.
Existing definitions should evolve only when doing so improves clarity without compromising the identity of the brand.
