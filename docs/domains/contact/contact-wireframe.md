## Desktop Composition

The Contact scene uses the shared Container and a 12-column editorial grid.

- Left region: five-column identity/context block.
- Right region: six-column form beginning at column seven.
- The composition is intentionally asymmetric.
- The scene uses the canonical dark institutional environment with a restrained warm radial atmosphere.

## Mobile / Tablet Composition

The grid collapses into natural document order:

1. Editorial context
2. Form
3. Shared Footer

The form remains fully functional without requiring hover behavior. Subject selection exposes the contextual description after selection/tap.

## Form Visual Language

Fields use editorial line treatments rather than heavy boxed controls. Gold communicates selected/focused emphasis according to the canonical design tokens. Success and error feedback use semantic design-system colors.

## Motion Sequence

The initial scene entrance is restrained:

1. Eyebrow
2. First title line
3. Italic second title line
4. Introductory copy
5. Form as one unit

No parallax, scrubbed entrance or decorative field-by-field cascade is required.

## Responsive Intent

Contact must remain compact enough to feel like a single purposeful encounter while allowing natural page height on smaller viewports. No fixed-height behavior may clip the submission action.

## Status

Wireframe v1.0 reflects the approved and implemented Contact experience and is frozen.
