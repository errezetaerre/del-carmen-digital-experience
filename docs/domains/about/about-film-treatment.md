# Title

**The Encounter**

---

# Purpose

The Encounter is the audiovisual synthesis of the Del Carmen About experience.

It is a cinematic art piece, not a technology commercial, corporate presentation or artist promotional reel.

Its purpose is to express the relationship between matter, observation, human gesture, painting and digital experience.

The film must preserve the principle:

Art first.  
Humanity second.  
Technology last.

# Narrative Structure

Four acts:

ACT I --- MATTER --- 00:00–00:15

ACT II --- CREATION --- 00:15–00:31

ACT III --- THE ENCOUNTER --- 00:31–00:49

ACT IV --- HUMAN --- 00:49–01:00

Narrative movement:

Matter → Tool → Gesture → Pigment → Painting → Artwork → Digital Experience → Human

Conceptual movement:

Silence → Matter → Gesture → Intention

The film begins documentary and physical, becomes progressively perceptual / digital, and ultimately returns to the human and physical artwork.

# Acts III and IV

The approved high-level direction is preserved:

ACT III develops the transition from physical painting into perceptual / digital experience.

ACT IV returns the experience toward the human and physical artwork.

These acts must preserve the circular concept:

Matter → Human → Art → Technology → Human → Art

The final detailed edit may be resolved during audiovisual production, but it must not introduce a different narrative premise without a treatment revision.

The closing conceptual statement may use:

`WHAT REMAINS IS HUMAN.`

followed by the Del Carmen identity.

# Authentic Media Foundation

Authentic studio material is the narrative foundation.

Approved source categories include:

- palette and pigment
- real paint mixing
- real hand painting
- Epifanía Nupcial process
- orchid / surface details
- studio environment
- artwork reveal
- restrained exhibition / artwork context where editorially justified

The film should not become a catalogue of many artworks.

Epifanía Nupcial remains the principal artwork protagonist of the current treatment.

---

# AI-Assisted Media

Seedance 2.5 is approved as the principal generative-video tool for this treatment where generative extension is required.

AI may support:

- motion
- atmosphere
- transitions
- visual extension
- perceptual transformation
- depth
- abstract movement from pigment toward light / space

AI must not:

- fabricate biographical events
- replace authentic artistic process where real footage exists
- make paintings literally “come alive”
- animate flowers as characters
- turn the film into a technology demonstration

The desired transformation is perceptual:

Pigment → Texture → Light → Space

Final edit, rhythm, typography, sound and color remain under Del Carmen art direction.

---

# Editing Principles

- cinematic rather than promotional
- slow enough to observe
- no unnecessary shot density
- no conventional ad pacing
- match cuts and material transitions are preferred
- digital transformation should emerge from the physical material
- return to physical/human presence before closure
- no CTA inside the film
